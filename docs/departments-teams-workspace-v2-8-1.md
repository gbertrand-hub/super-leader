# Super Leader V2.8.1 — Departments & Teams Workspace

## Objective

Transform the Departments & Teams page into a true management workspace that uses the available screen width and keeps the selected team visible while the directory is browsed.

## Main changes

- Structure KPI cards at the top of the page.
- Collapsible new-team form.
- Search by team, department or manager.
- Filters for department, lifecycle status and manager assignment.
- Sticky directory panel with independent scrolling on desktop.
- Detailed team workspace on the right.
- Active and archived teams remain clearly separated.
- The first active team opens automatically when the page is accessed without a team identifier.

## Permissions

The redesign does not change permissions:

- Owner, Admin and HR retain structural management permissions according to the existing permission helpers.
- Managers continue to see only the teams officially assigned to them.
- Existing audit history and team lifecycle actions are preserved.

## Database

No database migration is required.
